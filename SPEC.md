1. Project Overview

Langport requires a modern educational website that serves two primary customer journeys: new customers discovering and joining Langport, and current students accessing their learning experience. A third administrative layer should support Langport’s internal management needs.

Primary Users

·        New Customers / Website Visitors

·        Current Langport Students

·        Langport Administration / Academic & Customer Service Team

Main Objectives

·        Present Langport and its programs professionally.

·        Help new customers identify the right program and level.

·        Support placement, registration and payment workflows.

·        Provide current students with a clear Student Portal.

·        Allow students to access schedules, materials, assignments, attendance and progress.

·        Provide an Admin Dashboard as part of the planned system.

21. Responsive Design

The website and Student Portal must be fully responsive and optimized for mobile, tablet and desktop. The Student Dashboard should be especially easy to use on mobile.

22. Design Direction

·        Professional, modern, educational and friendly.

·        Modern EdTech feel rather than a traditional school website.

·        Clean layouts and generous spacing.

·        Rounded cards and clear icons.

·        Strong, consistent CTA buttons.

·        Simple navigation.

·        Consistent typography and Langport brand identity.

·        Use screenshots / UI previews where they improve understanding.

25. Core Product Principle

The website should be built around three simple user needs:

“I’m new here.” Help me choose.

“I’m already a student.” Help me learn.

“I manage Langport.” Help me manage.

The final experience should make Langport feel like a complete learning platform—not only a marketing website.
